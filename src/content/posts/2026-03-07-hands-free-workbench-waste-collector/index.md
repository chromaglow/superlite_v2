# Hands-Free Workbench Waste Collector

&nbsp;

I'm constantly faced with the challenge of managing the inevitable mess that comes with my work. From removing supports and infill to building jigs, the core of creating is often a waste intensive affair. In search of a solution to this problem, I decided to create a custom trash bin that would seamlessly integrate with my workbench setup.

&nbsp;

The goals for this project were to minimize the footprint on my workstation while maximizing the capacity to corral the waste and debris generated during construction. The solution I devised was a trash bin that hugs the edge of my workbench, with arms extending onto the desk itself to help funnel any errant materials into the container.

&nbsp;

As I explored different design approaches, I found that the more angular and chamfered second version, printed without the need for support structures, actually didn't quite align with my personal preferences. When it came to the hands-on experience, I actually gravitated more towards the curved version, which had a more organic and visually appealing aesthetic.

&nbsp;

This project has been an iterative process, and I foresee one more version in the works. By constantly refining the design and addressing the practical challenges of my workspace, I'm confident that I'll land on a final solution that not only helps me tame the chaos of my maker activities but also seamlessly integrates into the overall flow and aesthetic of my workbench setup.

&nbsp;

The beauty of this project lies in its simplicity and functionality. By leveraging a few key design principles and a thorough understanding of my own needs, I was able to create a custom tool that enhances my productivity and keeps my workstation clean and organized.
